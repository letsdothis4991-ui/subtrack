# SubTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

SubTrack is a subscription tracker for Android and the web. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

## Data you enter
Subscriptions, categories, budgets, notes, check-ins and settings are stored **on your device** (or, for the web app, in your browser's storage). On Android they may also be in your personal Google device backup if you have it turned on.

## Optional account and sync
If you sign in to sync, we store your **email address** and a copy of your SubTrack data in our database (hosted by Supabase) so it can appear on your other devices. Only you can read it: access is restricted to your signed-in account. You can delete your account and the cloud copy at any time in **Settings → Sync → Delete account**; data on your devices stays until you erase it.

## Smart add (SubTrack Plus)
When you use smart add, the screenshot or text you choose is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and then to Anthropic's Claude API to extract subscription details. We don't keep the screenshot or text after the request finishes; we keep only a count of requests to enforce a monthly limit. Anthropic processes the content under its [commercial terms and privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and does not use API data to train its models. Don't send screenshots that contain information you don't want processed.

## Exchange rates
The app downloads public exchange rates from the Frankfurter API (api.frankfurter.app). The request contains no personal data.

## Advertising (Android app)
The free Android app shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, and, where you consent, to personalise them. Users in the EEA, UK and regulated US states are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change their choice at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**. See [how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). SubTrack Plus removes ads. The web app shows no ads.

## Purchases
SubTrack Plus is processed by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that the purchase exists; we never see your payment details.

## Notifications
Renewal reminders and the monthly check-in are scheduled locally on your device.

## Children
SubTrack is not directed at children under 13.
