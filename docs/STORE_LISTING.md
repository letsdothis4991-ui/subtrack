**App name:** SubTrack – Subscription Tracker

**Short description (80 chars):**
Track subscriptions, get reminded before renewals, and see where your money goes.

**Full description:**

Know exactly what you pay for, and cancel what you don't use before it renews.

SubTrack is a simple, private subscription tracker. No bank login, no account: just add your subscriptions and SubTrack does the maths.

• See your total per month and per year, in your own currency
• Get a reminder days before each renewal or free trial ends
• Add popular services in one tap, with a direct link to manage or cancel
• Sort spending into categories: streaming, news, AI, utilities and your own
• Charts: spend by category, monthly history and the months ahead
• Set a monthly budget, overall or per category
• Note why you have each subscription ("watching The Bear") so it's easy to decide what to drop
• Keep a history of past subscriptions and what they cost you
• Track shared and family plans: split costs, see who owes you, count only your share
• Subscriptions in any currency, converted with daily exchange rates
• Monthly "still using it?" check-in that shows you what to cancel
• Price-rise alerts, plus how much you'd save on annual plans
• Settle up shared plans: see who's paid and send a friendly reminder
• Home-screen widget with your monthly total and next renewals
• Optional free sync between your phone and the web app
• Import from a CSV file. With Plus: paste a billing email and SubTrack adds the subscriptions for you

No bank login needed. A small banner keeps SubTrack free. SubTrack Plus is one payment, no subscription: no ads, a renewal calendar, your year in review, colour themes and widget styles.
