# SubTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

SubTrack is a subscription tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data you enter
Subscriptions, categories, budgets, notes and settings are stored **only on your device** (or, for the web app, in your browser's local storage). We don't run servers that receive this data, and there is no account. On Android your data may be included in your personal Google device backup if you have it turned on. Exported backup files go only where you choose to save or share them.

## Exchange rates
The app downloads public currency exchange rates from the Frankfurter API (api.frankfurter.app). That request contains no personal data.

## Advertising (Android app)
The free Android app shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, and, where you consent, to personalise them. Users in the EEA, UK and regulated US states are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and can change their choice at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**. See [how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

The web app shows no ads.

## Purchases
The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase exists; we never see your payment details.

## Notifications
Renewal reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to a server.

## Children
SubTrack is not directed at children under 13.
