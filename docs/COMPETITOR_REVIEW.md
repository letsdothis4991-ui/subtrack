# Competitor review: the top 5 subscription trackers

Researched October 2026. Many "best of" lists are written by app vendors, so these notes stick to features several sources agree on.

| App | Platforms | Model | What it's known for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Rocket Money** | iOS, Android, web | Free tier; premium about $7–14/mo | Links to your bank and **detects subscriptions automatically**; can cancel subscriptions for you; budgeting. Trade-off: gives a third party bank access |
| **Bobby** | iOS only | Free, one-off ~$3 unlock | The clean, manual tracker people copy: **pre-built entries for popular services**, colourful cards, totals |
| **ReSubs** | iOS, Android | Free tier (no ads), Premium | **Privacy-first**, never connects to your bank; CSV import, Gmail receipt scanning, AI extraction from screenshots and billing emails |
| **Tilla** | Android | Free for 5 subs; $2.99 lifetime | Cheap lifetime unlock; analytics, cloud sync, local backups |
| **Subscriptions** (Android) | Android | Free | A Bobby-style app for Android: price, billing frequency, **payment method**, monthly totals, category tags |

## What SubTrack takes from each

| Idea | From | In SubTrack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-tap popular services | Bobby | 45 presets with colour, category and **manage-account link** |
| Payment method, categories, monthly total | Subscriptions | ✅ |
| No bank access, local-first | ReSubs, Bobby | ✅ no account; data stays on device |
| Backups, export | Tilla, ReSubs | JSON backup/restore and CSV export |
| Cheap one-off unlock instead of a subscription | Bobby, Tilla | "Remove ads" one-off purchase |
| Help cancelling | Rocket Money | Manage-account link on every subscription and in reminders |
| Budgets | Rocket Money | Overall and per-category monthly budgets |
| Web access | Rocket Money | Same app runs on the web |

## Where SubTrack goes further than the manual trackers

- **Per-subscription currency plus a base currency** with live ECB rates (most manual trackers use one currency)
- **Past subscriptions with real history**: start/end dates, price changes over time, total paid
- **"Why I have it" notes** shown when you're about to cancel
- **Shared subscriptions**: split, I pay (shows what you're owed), or someone else pays, with totals that count only your share
- **Charts of actual monthly charges** (yearly plans show in the month they hit) plus 6 months projected, not just an average

## Gaps closed in v1.1

- Automatic detection: smart add reads pasted billing emails (like ReSubs) without bank access
- Cloud sync between phone and web (like Tilla and Rocket Money)
- Service logos and a home-screen widget
- Still to come: iOS release, web push reminders
